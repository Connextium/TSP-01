TSP-01 / RESEARCH METHODOLOGY

Research standards and scope.

TSP-01 studies tokenized financial rights, real-world assets, capital formation, circulation and accountable financial execution. The publication distinguishes reported evidence, experimental results and proposed system designs.

01 / RESEARCH ORGANIZATION

Three domains, nine chapters.

The Instruments & Assets domain examines token representations, custodial backing and settlement instruments. Capital & Markets examines the sources, circulation and return of value. Infrastructure & Assurance covers ledger coordination, institutional control and autonomous governance. Monetary and stablecoin issues are included only where they affect the asset and capital lifecycle.

02 / EVIDENCE HIERARCHY

Evidence is not the same as inference.

SOURCE-BASED

Published research

External papers, working papers, policy and technical reports are cited by source. Their findings remain attributable to their authors, with appropriate caveats.

EXPERIMENTAL

Proofs of concept

Technical project reports illustrate tested feasibility under specific assumptions, not universal deployment, financial performance or regulatory approval.

TSP-01 DESIGN

Hypotheses and models

Architectures, diagrams and financial numerical cases are original research proposals and synthetic illustrations unless explicitly identified as observed results.

03 / RESEARCH WORKFLOW

From claim to financial proof.

  1. Identify a precise financial question and the underlying legally enforceable right or obligation.
  2. Review primary-source theory, experimental architectures and documented limitations.
  3. Define the institutional actors, asset and money records, settlement boundary and financial-state invariants.
  4. Use an explicit numerical example to distinguish economic flows from token events.
  5. Document failure modes, alternative explanations, open assumptions and falsifiable tests.
  6. Map the results into Capital DLC–ACTOR and the six experimental workstreams without claiming commercial deployment.
04 / SOURCES AND REVIEW LIMITS

Source transparency.

References prioritize BIS, CPMI, IMF, FSB, IOSCO, primary technical project reports and identified token standards. A selection of files from the supplied Drive research folder informed the initial source inventory; the collection has not undergone exhaustive paper-by-paper verification. Legal, quantitative and implementation claims require case-specific verification before operational use. The programme contains technical analyses, not regulated financial products or investment recommendations.
