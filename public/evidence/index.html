Research integrity

Reading status and limitations

  • The supplied folder was publicly accessible and a selection of PDFs was downloaded. This review does not claim exhaustive reading of all files and subfolders.
  • BIS Innovation Hub projects are experiments. They do not establish industry-wide deployment, market adoption, final legal standing or central-bank commitment to an instrument.
  • Working papers express their authors’ analysis; conclusions may differ from institutional positions.
  • The experimental scenarios on TSP-01 are proposed tests, not historical observations or real-money performance claims.
