01 / Financial-structure thesis

The economic question

Permissioned networks and cryptographic signatures alone do not establish legal rights, complete records or regulated financial integrity.
TSP-01 conceptual architecture. Labels indicate distinct evidence or control boundaries; not a measured project result.
02 / Research scope

Mechanisms and constraints

  • Eligibility, limits, risk policy, sanctions and investor permissions
  • Issuer/custodian/agent segregation of duties and governance rights
  • Independent verification of funds, tokens, liabilities and journal populations
  • Data confidentiality, selective disclosure, privacy and audit access
  • Upgrade governance, cybersecurity, liquidity runs, disruption and operational recovery
03 / Failure analysis

What can go wrong?

A token transaction is cryptographically valid but unauthorized, legally improper or not captured in reconciliation.
Falsification test / proposed

Experimental proof obligation

Inject unauthorized mint, stale identity, missing audit events and partial custody backing; verify detection and remediation.

Control model

Ledger + economic state

Model participant roles, monetary or asset liabilities, valid instructions, finality evidence and independent accounting reconstruction. Distinguish assumptions from measured outcomes.
