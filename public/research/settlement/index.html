01 / Financial-structure thesis

The economic question

Atomic exchange, synchronized payment infrastructures and asynchronous hybrid systems require different proofs and contingency designs.
TSP-01 conceptual architecture. Labels indicate distinct evidence or control boundaries; not a measured project result.
02 / Research scope

Mechanisms and constraints

  • DvP and PvP payment/asset synchronization
  • Legal finality, confirmation, settlement instruction, discharge and conditional hold
  • Shared programmable platform versus RTGS-connected synchronization
  • Cross-border operating hours, recipient bank funding and clearing
  • Failed legs, partial fills, fallback, reversals, exceptions and auditability
03 / Failure analysis

What can go wrong?

An asset leg is finalized while the monetary leg remains incomplete and unprotected.
Falsification test / proposed

Experimental proof obligation

Run the same obligation in atomic-ledger, Meridian-style synchronized, and asynchronous hybrid settlement models.

Control model

Ledger + economic state

Model participant roles, monetary or asset liabilities, valid instructions, finality evidence and independent accounting reconstruction. Distinguish assumptions from measured outcomes.
