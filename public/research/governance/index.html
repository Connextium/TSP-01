01 / Financial-structure thesis

The economic question

Agents may propose or execute financial operations only under explicit, revocable, recorded and enforceable mandates.
Research hypothesis: autonomous operations are only permitted inside a versioned mandate and enforceable approval boundary.
02 / Research scope

Mechanisms and constraints

  • Governance authority, responsibility and versioned financial policy
  • Agent lifecycle: observe → model → propose → negotiate → approve → execute → reconcile
  • Bounded trading, treasury and sourcing mandates with counterparty and spend limits
  • Dual-principal autonomy, safe negotiation, human review and emergency stops
  • Decision provenance, risk monitoring, auditability and verified accounting outcomes
03 / Failure analysis

What can go wrong?

An AI agent executes a plausible but off-mandate settlement, leaving no approval record or recoverable position.
Falsification test / proposed

Experimental proof obligation

Simulate a treasury agent with signed limits; reject stale FX rates, over-budget trades and unauthorized counterparties.

Control model

Ledger + economic state

Model participant roles, monetary or asset liabilities, valid instructions, finality evidence and independent accounting reconstruction. Distinguish assumptions from measured outcomes.
