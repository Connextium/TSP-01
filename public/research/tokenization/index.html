01 / Financial-structure thesis

The economic question

A token can model an instrument and its transfer rules without itself creating legal title, a liability or redeemability.
TSP-01 conceptual architecture. Labels indicate distinct evidence or control boundaries; not a measured project result.
02 / Research scope

Mechanisms and constraints

  • Native digital issuance, asset mirrors, bearer claims and custodial receipts
  • Fungibility, semi-fungibility and ERC-20 / ERC-1155 / ERC-6909 / ERC-3525 / permissioned standards
  • Canonical registry, eligibility, transfer restrictions, beneficial ownership and administrator control
  • Issue → verify → mint → transfer → encumber → redeem → burn, including disputes and corrections
  • Backing supply, legal evidence and independent state reconstruction
03 / Failure analysis

What can go wrong?

Duplicate tokenized claims or a burn that fails to extinguish the underlying right.
Falsification test / proposed

Experimental proof obligation

Represent the same verified obligation under three token standards; reconcile rights, tokens and redemptions at every step.

Control model

Ledger + economic state

Model participant roles, monetary or asset liabilities, valid instructions, finality evidence and independent accounting reconstruction. Distinguish assumptions from measured outcomes.
