01 / Financial-structure thesis

The economic question

Central-bank liabilities, bank-deposit tokens and stablecoins are economically distinct, even when all circulate as digital tokens.
TSP-01 conceptual architecture. Labels indicate distinct evidence or control boundaries; not a measured project result.
02 / Research scope

Mechanisms and constraints

  • Two-tier money, sovereign settlement and central-bank reserves
  • Deposit-token liability and bank balance-sheet treatment
  • Reserve-backed stablecoin asset composition, redemption and liquidity queues
  • Issuance, transfer, issuer restrictions and final redemption
  • Deposit substitution, liquidity contagion, issuer failure and legal protection
03 / Failure analysis

What can go wrong?

Blockchain confirmation is incorrectly treated as unconditional par redemption or sovereign finality.
Falsification test / proposed

Experimental proof obligation

Compare issuance, transfer and stressed redemption of a bank token, stablecoin and central bank money instrument.

Control model

Ledger + economic state

Model participant roles, monetary or asset liabilities, valid instructions, finality evidence and independent accounting reconstruction. Distinguish assumptions from measured outcomes.
