01 / Financial-structure thesis

The economic question

Interoperability requires consistent financial semantics and external settlement proofs, not merely a blockchain bridge.
TSP-01 conceptual architecture. Labels indicate distinct evidence or control boundaries; not a measured project result.
02 / Research scope

Mechanisms and constraints

  • Public, permissioned, consortium and conventional database ledgers
  • Governance, validators, trust anchors, consensus and privacy boundaries
  • ISO 20022 business messages and multi-ledger state mappings
  • Bridges, synchronization operators, events, claims and settlement finality
  • Complete reconstruction of bank and token state with independently audited evidence
03 / Failure analysis

What can go wrong?

A technically correct cross-chain transfer changes token balances but leaves economic claims inconsistent.
Falsification test / proposed

Experimental proof obligation

Compare ISO 20022 payment, ledger token transfer and double-entry posting with duplicate, missing and delayed events.

Control model

Ledger + economic state

Model participant roles, monetary or asset liabilities, valid instructions, finality evidence and independent accounting reconstruction. Distinguish assumptions from measured outcomes.
