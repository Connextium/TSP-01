01 / Financial-structure thesis

The economic question

Transfer fees are only one part of a payment or securities execution; executable rates and spendable proceeds are decisive.
TSP-01 conceptual architecture. Labels indicate distinct evidence or control boundaries; not a measured project result.
02 / Research scope

Mechanisms and constraints

  • Cross-border costs: bank, fintech, stablecoin and hybrid rails
  • FX RFQ/dealers, order books, AMMs, bridges and market-maker balance sheets
  • Pool depth, spreads, slippage, funding and prefunding
  • Continuous tokenized share markets and deviation from underlying reference quotes
  • Local-currency ramps, recipient accessibility and stress redemption
03 / Failure analysis

What can go wrong?

An apparently cheap on-chain transfer produces an expensive or unusable beneficiary payout.
Falsification test / proposed

Experimental proof obligation

Compare all-in cross-border costs with quote, liquidity, network, funding and fiat conversion components.

Control model

Ledger + economic state

Model participant roles, monetary or asset liabilities, valid instructions, finality evidence and independent accounting reconstruction. Distinguish assumptions from measured outcomes.
