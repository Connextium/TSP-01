01 / Financial-structure thesis

The economic question

Financial capital, recorded ownership and circulating tokens take different routes, especially in secondary markets.
Proposed research model: reconcile funding, underlying assets, registered claims, token supply and economic proceeds separately.
02 / Research scope

Mechanisms and constraints

  • Capital commitment, subscriptions, primary issuance and cash allocation
  • Treasury funding and acquisition of underlying real-world assets
  • Token issuance, investor holdings, rights changes and transfer agents
  • Dealer inventory and secondary trade proceeds—not additional issuer fundraising
  • Dividends, repayments, liquidation, redemptions and audited closing of positions
03 / Failure analysis

What can go wrong?

Secondary token turnover is confused with new investment capital raised by the issuer.
Falsification test / proposed

Experimental proof obligation

Trace $100k investment, asset acquisition, $25k secondary trade and $4k income distribution through separate ledgers.

Control model

Ledger + economic state

Model participant roles, monetary or asset liabilities, valid instructions, finality evidence and independent accounting reconstruction. Distinguish assumptions from measured outcomes.
