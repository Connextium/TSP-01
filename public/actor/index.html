01 / Capital and entitlement pathways

Follow cash, rights and settlement

Proposed research model: reconcile funding, underlying assets, registered claims, token supply and economic proceeds separately.

Every ACTOR stage identifies a responsible institution, a claim or monetary liability, an authorization, a state change and the evidence needed to verify that state. Asset and cash movements may occur on different platforms.

02 / Worked conceptual case

Custody-backed securities receipt

ACTOR stageCapital / token actionEvidence & institution
ActivateInvestor signs a subscription and passes suitability checksIssuer records accepted investment terms
ChannelFunds move into a segregated collection or issuance accountTreasury matches investor funding and currency
TransformIssuer acquires backing shares or funds; receipt supply is mintedCustodian verifies holdings; asset rights are allocated
OrchestrateDividend is received, allocated and reconciled to eligible holdersServicing events reconcile custody, issuer and ledger
ReturnInvestor sells, redeems or receives distributionsProceeds reach spendable account and receipt claims close
A token event is not by itself proof that the underlying share was bought, a cash leg settled, or a holder is legally entitled to proceeds.
