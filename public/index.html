RESEARCH PROGRAMME / ARCHITECTURE

Capital architecture, not digital-money catalogues

Tokenization is meaningful when claims, monetary instruments, markets, settlement and institutions fit together. Explore nine linked research chapters organized in three financial domains.

Representation ≠ entitlement. Token transfer ≠ usable funds. Technical execution ≠ legal settlement. Automated decisions ≠ institutional authority.
