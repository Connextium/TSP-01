EXPERIMENTAL RESEARCH / CROSS-DOMAIN

Six adaptable workstreams.

SPE, TRR, TLS, TSR, AFG and RPI are cross-cutting experimental capabilities. They adapt to the nine research chapters without losing their established identities.

Capability definitions

Settlement Protocol Execution

TRR

Token Representation Registry

Canonical rights, lifecycle and supply assurance

TLS

Treasury & Liquidity Structures

FX, netting, issuer and funding economics

TSR

Tokenized Securities Receipts

Custodial backing, depository claims and securities servicing

AFG

Agentic Finance & Governance

Institutional policy, risk limits, agent authorization and oversight

RPI

ISO 20022, cross-ledger semantic state and bank rails
