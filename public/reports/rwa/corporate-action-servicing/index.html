Corporate Actions and Fractional Securities: Entitlement and Servicing

Record dates, dividends, splits, cash-in-lieu and the beneficial ownership register

Executive abstract

Fractional token units do not eliminate corporate action administration. Dividends, splits, votes, spin-offs, tender offers and withholding each require accurate holder snapshots and custody-to-issuer reconciliation. A depositary receipt holder may have proportionate economic interests without standing as registered shareholder. This report specifies how to allocate rights without duplicating or silently extinguishing them.

01 / SOURCE-BASED RESEARCH

Published evidence and limits.

Source-based observations are distinguished from TSP-01 design proposals. BIS Innovation Hub prototypes demonstrate specified experiments; they are not evidence of generalized market deployment.

E01

Asset servicing is integral to tokenized capital markets

Project Guardian's Operationalising Tokenised Funds framework addresses share-register models, fund operations and asset servicing rather than simply token transfer. IMF research evaluates tokenization across origination, trading, post-trade and administration. The implication is that an asset token without complete servicing and exception handling is an incomplete financial instrument.

E02

Holder status determines entitlements

The SEC's 2026 nonbinding staff statement distinguishes direct tokenized securities and third-party arrangements. A receipt holder's pass-through rights depend on the depositary contract, while voting and dividends flow through legally recognized chain-of-title and record-date rules. A token transfer block timestamp alone may not determine the economic holder during unsettled trades or custody record corrections.

E03

Market price does not settle distribution rights

A token may trade when its reference exchange is closed, with spot prices reflecting order-book liquidity and other risks. That trading price does not allocate dividends or correct a split, and it cannot resolve the custodian's final cash receipts. The FSB discusses vulnerabilities from asynchronous or fragmented token markets. A receipt platform must distinguish indicative market prices from the legally defined entitlement schedule.

02 / TSP-01 ANALYTICAL ARCHITECTURE

Mechanisms and responsibilities.

Original TSP-01 conceptual process model. It is an analytical illustration, not empirical proof of a deployed system.
M01

Corporate action event taxonomy

Track notification, confirmation, ex-date, record date, effective date, payment date and any election deadline as separate events, each linked to the official instrument identifier and trusted source. Create an immutable versioned event and record approved corrections. The token register may need a freeze, lock or post-event adjustment, but retroactive changes require formal authority.

M02

Beneficial owner and entitlement snapshot

Determine eligible holders by the legally applicable record-date rules, not merely by a current wallet query. Disputes can arise from pending settlement, omnibus custody, lending, wrapped tokens or transfers in flight. The entitlement calculation should use a frozen, independently reconstructible population and a prescribed treatment of encumbered or unsettled interests.

M03

Gross-to-net dividend accounting

Let gross distribution G be the custodian's amount received; let W denote legally required withholding, F fees and R rounding or unclaimed liabilities. Amount available to be paid to holders equals G − W − F − R, with unpaid amounts preserved as recognized liabilities rather than disappearing as dust. Each investor entitlement must reconcile to a payable and settlement voucher.

M04

Stock splits and rights conversions

A split alters underlying share count and the entitlement conversion ratio or receipt supply in a contractually specified way; it should not by itself create economic wealth. Spin-offs and mergers may replace the underlying identifier, create cash consideration or impose holder elections. The issuer must record those changes independently and verify receipt supply after the action before reopening transfer.

Two systems of record. Dotted vertical links require explicit legal, accounting and settlement reconciliation—not presumed on-chain equivalence.
03 / WORKED FINANCIAL SCENARIO · SYNTHETIC

Synthetic dividend, withholding and split

A depositary holds 500 shares supporting 5,000 receipts. The share issuer declares a USD 2.00 dividend. For illustration assume a uniform 15% withholding rate and zero fees; actual tax depends on the instrument, jurisdiction and holder.

Calculation / invariant

1,000 gross = 150 withholding + 850 net. Net/receipt = 850 / 5,000 = 0.17. A 3:2 split changes share units, not mechanically the holder's economic wealth.

The split scenario assumes the receipt ratio remains fixed and the issuer is contractually authorized to adjust the supply; the alternative is adjusting the ratio and leaving receipts unchanged.

06 / CONCLUSION

Research implication.

Fractional access is not a complete product unless corporate-action entitlements can be administered and independently verified. TSR should model servicing, withholding and record-date transformations as first-class financial state changes.
