Autonomous Treasury and Financial Intelligence That Reconcile

Liquidity headroom, spending reservations, quote freshness and verified journal outcomes

Executive abstract

Autonomous treasury must make decisions under cash, obligation and counterparty constraints that are not visible in a wallet balance alone. This report links AI route recommendations to deterministic liquidity reservations, settlement evidence and double-entry accounting. It distinguishes theoretical route optimization from actions that can safely discharge a financial obligation.

01 / SOURCE-BASED RESEARCH

Published evidence and limits.

Source-based observations are distinguished from TSP-01 design proposals. BIS Innovation Hub prototypes demonstrate specified experiments; they are not evidence of generalized market deployment.

E01

Runtime controls are necessary for financial action

MAS SAFR recommends identifying agent authority, checking actions at runtime and preserving decision evidence. An agent can suggest an FX route, but a policy gateway must evaluate available cash and existing commitments immediately before execution. Delegating this to the language model's own memory leaves the institution exposed to stale balances, race conditions or prompt-injected exceptions.

E02

Liquidity and settlement have independent evidence

BIS Rialto, Meridian FX and Agorá examine combinations of FX conversion, synchronization and programmable payment infrastructure. Their prototypes reinforce why treasury must distinguish a quote, conditional hold, final transfer and beneficiary spendability. An autonomous agent can select among eligible routes, but only independently controlled financial systems can report actual reserves and final payments.

E03

Accounting verifies rather than narrates the outcome

IMF research on tokenized finance and FSB risk studies highlight the governance and operational dependencies of interconnected digital financial systems. A model-generated success message is not a bank statement, validated custody position or accounting posting. The system should record true executed amounts, fees, FX differences, reversals and unresolved claims under a canonical intent ID.

02 / TSP-01 ANALYTICAL ARCHITECTURE

Mechanisms and responsibilities.

Original TSP-01 conceptual process model. It is an analytical illustration, not empirical proof of a deployed system.
M01

Calculate spendable liquidity

Track settled cash, required operating buffer, reserved outgoing payments, collateral calls and permitted incoming liquidity. SpendableHeadroom = SettledAvailable − MinimumBuffer − ReservedPayments − CollateralHolds. Mark projected incoming funds and unredeemed settlement tokens separately rather than counting them as instantly spendable bank cash. Currency-specific positions and permissible conversion limits are necessary for cross-border treasury decisions.

M02

Proposal and reservation

An agent can rank eligible routes based on net proceeds, FX quote depth, funding time and counterparty risk. After a deterministic controller approves the route, reserve the cash against one immutable intent ID to prevent double spending by parallel agents. Confirm rate and destination before signing. Expired quotes or revoked mandates should cause reauthorization, not retries with stale terms.

M03

Settlement and journal closure

An executed payment should produce identifiable bank or token settlement evidence and matching journals for cash, payables, fees and any FX result. If payment fails, the controller releases reservations only when the actual rail's state allows it. Independent reconciliation should detect mismatched fees, stale settlements and payments whose beneficiary received a different amount from what the agent predicted.

03 / WORKED FINANCIAL SCENARIO · SYNTHETIC

Synthetic treasury headroom check

Settled USD cash $300,000; required buffer $100,000; already reserved outgoing payments $70,000. The agent proposes an additional $180,000 conversion.

EventActor / basisFinancial movementRecord or control
Available bank balanceBank$300,000Authenticated settled cash
Minimum bufferTreasury policy− $100,000Must not be used for discretionary transfer
Outstanding reservationsSettlement coordinator− $70,000Already committed
Permitted headroomPolicy engine$130,000300,000 − 100,000 − 70,000
Agent proposalAgent$180,000Rejected: exceeds remaining headroom by $50,000
Calculation / invariant

SpendableHeadroom = $300,000 − $100,000 − $70,000 = $130,000. Proposal $180,000 > $130,000, so policy must reject or resize it before any financial instruction.

The agent does not create funding through optimization. Even a smaller instruction requires valid counterparty, quote, fee budget and approval checks.

06 / CONCLUSION

Research implication.

AFG is most valuable when financial intelligence is coupled to true balances, mandate controls and settled evidence. Autonomous treasury should be a governed decision-to-journal process rather than an unconstrained portfolio agent.
