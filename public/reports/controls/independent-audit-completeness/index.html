REPORT 08.02III · Infrastructure & AssuranceTechnical reportRESEARCH EDITION / 2026

Independent Financial-State Verification: Completeness, Valuation and Reconciliation

Proof that the known token ledger is not missing obligations, backing assets or unsettled positions

Executive abstract

Blockchains can make transactions tamper-evident while leaving the population of underlying claims invisible. Financial assurance must independently establish completeness of obligations, legitimacy of custodial backing, valuation and contractual discharge. This report develops a three-record audit model—rights registry, token ledger and monetary/custody evidence—with controls for mismatches that a purely on-chain balance check would overlook.

01 / SOURCE-BASED RESEARCH

Published evidence and limits.

Source-based observations are distinguished from TSP-01 design proposals. BIS Innovation Hub prototypes demonstrate specified experiments; they are not evidence of generalized market deployment.

E01

Auditability is not the same as data visibility

The FSB and IMF analyses of tokenized finance identify operational risk, governance concentration and interconnectedness as material considerations. A visible transfer history does not identify unrecorded obligations or whether an off-chain security still exists. The auditor therefore needs a population-level assertion from an independent legal register, contract list, custodian or bank, not only the digital token issuer's own statements.

E02

Asset servicing produces off-chain evidence

Project Guardian's fund and collateral lifecycle frameworks include transfer agents, custody, asset administration and other operators that must account for legal and economic changes outside a token contract. A corporate action can change entitlements without changing wallet balances on the announcement date. Similarly, receipt burns do not necessarily prove asset delivery or final cash payments. An external evidence pipeline is a research requirement, not proof of existing market practice.

E03

Source authority and ledger semantics affect verification

The BIS Project Meridian illustrates coordinated asset transfer and money settlement among independently operated systems, while Project Agorá studies a shared platform with institutional roles. Neither structure eliminates the question of whether an authorized source reported all claims. Reconciliation should also identify statuses such as pending, rejected, final and legally disputed rather than treating every interface event as settled.

02 / TSP-01 ANALYTICAL ARCHITECTURE

Mechanisms and responsibilities.

Original TSP-01 conceptual process model. It is an analytical illustration, not empirical proof of a deployed system.
M01

Three distinct audit assertions

Existence: tokenized rights reported by the issuer correspond to valid underlying assets or claims. Completeness: all relevant legally outstanding claims are included, even if not already known to the token system. Rights and obligations: the amount, holder, currency, restrictions and seniority match valid documentation. A fourth financial assertion concerns settlement: discharged positions have final money or asset evidence and balancing accounts.

M02

Cross-system reconciliation equations

For a depositary receipt, eligible backing capacity minus token claims should be non-negative under the contract's conversion ratio. For a payment obligation, approved outstanding should equal approved opening balance plus valid increases minus final settlements and recognized cancellations. A token contract can supply one side of each equation, but another independent register must provide the economic side. Exceptions must be classified by timing, quantity, ownership or legal status.

M03

Event provenance and policy versions

Link each asset, token and monetary event to a unique economic-event ID and issuer/custodian/bank source. Preserve signed custody statements, bank confirmation IDs, legal instrument identifiers, authorized corporate action evidence, hash references, policy versions and correction history. Data quality rules should detect stale custody files, duplicate transfers, timestamp mismatches, missing holder records and unapproved manual adjustments.

M04

Assurance under privacy constraints

An independent reviewer may need proof of balance completeness without seeing every investor's confidential details. Provide encrypted or restricted evidence layers, aggregate reconciliation, authorized attestations and selective review of underlying contracts. A published Merkle root of the token list does not prove the issuer's liabilities are complete if undisclosed obligations were never placed in the source list.

03 / WORKED FINANCIAL SCENARIO · SYNTHETIC

Synthetic custody discrepancy audit

A custodian report shows 1,200 underlying shares, each supporting 10 receipts. The issuer reports 11,000 outstanding receipts. Of the custodied shares, 150 are independently identified as pledged to another secured obligation and cannot back freely redeemable receipts under the assumed contract.

Calculation / invariant

Eligible shares = 1,200 − 150 = 1,050. Eligible receipt capacity = 1,050 × 10 = 10,500. Outstanding receipts 11,000 ⇒ 500 receipt units of undercoverage.

A simplistic on-chain supply check could report 11,000 valid token units. The economically decisive discrepancy only appears after external custody and encumbrance evidence.

05 / EXPERIMENTS & FALSIFICATION

What needs testing.

  • T01

    Reconstruct outstanding rights from external asset and obligations registers, intentionally inserting a missing investor and an ineligible pledged unit.

  • T02

    Verify journal-to-bank settlement for both fully and partially redeemed claims, with replay attempts and corrective entries.

    Demonstrate rights verification under masked investor identifiers while preserving legally authorized audit disclosure.

06 / CONCLUSION

Research implication.

Independent financial assurance is a claim-completeness and rights problem, not a token explorer feature. TRR, TSR, SPE and AFG should share evidence protocols that enable an independent reviewer to detect omitted assets, overissued receipts, disputed claims and unrecognized settlements.
