Choosing Settlement Instruments for Tokenized Asset Transactions

Bank money, tokenized deposits, stablecoins and central-bank settlement in a supporting role

Executive abstract

TSP-01 is not a monetary-policy research site. It must nevertheless identify which asset discharges a transaction, what legal claim the payee receives, and when funds become usable. A tokenized receipt can be funded in commercial-bank money, a tokenized deposit, a private stablecoin or an institutionally accessible central-bank settlement arrangement. This briefing offers an instrument-selection method grounded in asset lifecycle requirements.

01 / SOURCE-BASED RESEARCH

Published evidence and limits.

Source-based observations are distinguished from TSP-01 design proposals. BIS Innovation Hub prototypes demonstrate specified experiments; they are not evidence of generalized market deployment.

E01

Several monetary instruments can support one asset token

The BIS Annual Economic Reports 2025 and 2026 distinguish central-bank money, commercial-bank liabilities and stablecoins as different monetary forms and explore how they might interact in programmable arrangements. The BIS/CPMI tokenisation framework makes this choice a part of the transaction design rather than an automatic consequence of the asset token standard. The financial question is not whether the settlement asset is on-chain, but whether its transfer discharges the obligation under the transaction contract.

E02

Prototype architectures are not required choices

BIS Project Agorá tested a programmable wholesale platform integrating bank deposits and central-bank reserves, while Helvetia studied tokenized asset settlement in a wholesale CBDC setting. These experiments illustrate feasible design options, not a mandate to replace all bank payment systems or issue a new currency. An asset platform should avoid making its legal and servicing layers dependent on any one payment token unless there is a deliberate governance reason.

02 / TSP-01 ANALYTICAL ARCHITECTURE

Mechanisms and responsibilities.

Original TSP-01 conceptual process model. It is an analytical illustration, not empirical proof of a deployed system.
M01

Instrument decision table

For each settlement instrument compare the issuer liability, eligible users, redemption rights, source of finality, transfer restrictions, supported currencies, operating hours, counterparty concentration and off-ramp. A stablecoin balance under a self-custodied wallet is not automatically the same claim as a credited insured bank deposit. A central-bank reserve transfer may have strong settlement properties but is generally accessible only through the relevant system's eligibility and operational conditions.

M02

Asset-payment interface

Define a PaymentLeg with amount, currency, payer, payee, instrument, settlement network, irrevocability criterion, expiry, fees and proof reference. A DeliveryLeg identifies the valid asset right being delivered. A coordinator links the two with conditional acceptance, cancellation and exception states. Recording chain confirmation without evidence that the payee received its contractual money obligation leaves the DvP assertion incomplete.

M03

Keep stablecoin research at the relevant boundary

Reserve management, bank-deposit substitution, monetary sovereignty, issuer portfolio dynamics and detailed macroeconomic consequences belong in a companion money edition. For TSP-01, retain only what changes a tokenized asset investment or trading decision: redeemability, credit exposure, liquidity availability, currency conversion, legal finality and operational reliability.

Calculation / invariant

Economic payment completion must be defined by the contract, not inferred from technology: AcceptedPayment = ValidInstrument ∧ FinalityEvidence ∧ BeneficiaryUsabilityCondition.

The same securities receipt may use different settlement arrangements. Asset rights and custody should remain well-defined regardless of the chosen funding rail.

05 / EXPERIMENTS & FALSIFICATION

What needs testing.

  • T01

    Use one securities delivery with four simulated payment legs and verify which satisfy contractual discharge.

  • T02

    Measure time to usable beneficiary funds rather than ledger confirmation time alone.

  • T03

    Simulate a stablecoin suspension and bank payment outage without changing the asset-rights record.

06 / CONCLUSION

Research implication.

Monetary infrastructure should be treated as an interface to tokenized capital markets. The instrument chosen matters for finality, credit exposure and liquidity, but it should not dominate TSP-01's research agenda.
