Token-Standard Selection for Financial Claims and Receipts

ERC-1155, ERC-6909, ERC-3525 and controlled securities interfaces in an institutional registry

Executive abstract

Choosing a token standard is an engineering and lifecycle decision—not a legal classification of the underlying instrument. The same claim may be technically representable as a fungible class, a token-by-ID balance, a semi-fungible slot, or a controlled securities token. The correct selection depends on holder granularity, legal assignment rules, encumbrances, lifecycle partitioning, transfer restrictions, and the economics of reconciliation.

01 / SOURCE-BASED RESEARCH

Published evidence and limits.

Source-based observations are distinguished from TSP-01 design proposals. BIS Innovation Hub prototypes demonstrate specified experiments; they are not evidence of generalized market deployment.

E01

Standards have different unit models

ERC-6909 defines a minimal multi-token interface with per-ID balances and operators. ERC-3525 introduces a slot-and-value model associated with token IDs, enabling partial values within related categories. ERC-1155 provides a mature multi-token ecosystem for fungible and non-fungible IDs. ERC-7518 and other regulated-asset proposals introduce additional compliant and authorization interfaces, but implementation status, dependencies and contract-level guarantees must be assessed separately. None of these standards alone performs KYC, insolvency segregation or beneficial-owner recordkeeping.

E02

Legal design is upstream of interface design

The SEC staff's 2026 treatment of tokenized securities underscores why investor rights and issuer arrangements must precede interface choice. Project Guardian's institutional frameworks likewise focus on the fund lifecycle, on-chain registers, custody and asset servicing rather than treating issuance as the whole product. Choosing token ID structures before defining the authoritative unit of ownership risks making a technical representation expensive or impossible to reconcile with the actual registrar.

E03

Ecosystem support versus financial invariants

The most gas-efficient standard is not necessarily the least costly institutional model. A minimal interface can decrease transfer overhead but require more custom access controls, indexers and corporate-action tooling. A semi-fungible standard can encode tranche or maturity attributes but complicate exchange and custodian integration. The proper decision compares total implementation and operating costs, threat surface, event semantics, auditability and lifecycle extensibility—not contract deployment cost alone.

02 / TSP-01 ANALYTICAL ARCHITECTURE

Mechanisms and responsibilities.

Original TSP-01 conceptual process model. It is an analytical illustration, not empirical proof of a deployed system.
M01

Representing the unit of entitlement

Before selecting ERC interfaces, define whether a single unit means one legal share, one USD of principal, one percentage of a fund, or one contractual claim whose value may change. Then define how rights split and merge, how beneficial holders are enumerated, and whether the rights registry accepts transfer decisions by wallet alone. For controlled securities a token may represent a distinct transferable position and still require registrar validation and issuer-controlled restrictions.

M02

ERC-1155 / ERC-6909: class balance patterns

A class token model maintains balances keyed by token type or series. ERC-1155's multi-token conventions can be advantageous for wallets and marketplace integrations; ERC-6909 offers a minimal interface for multiple types. Financial risks arise when a single ID aggregates legally different claims—such as series with different liens or redemption dates—or when arbitrary fractional units hide a varying conversion ratio. Use immutable class metadata and a versioned entitlement registry.

M03

ERC-3525: slot and value are not a substitute for servicing

The slot/value model suits partially transferable positions grouped by shared characteristics, such as maturity or coupon schedule. However, value transfers do not automatically update the bond register, payment agent, collateral encumbrance ledger or eligibility restrictions. Assign a legally meaningful instrument ID and avoid treating a slot as proof of all associated legal terms. If a position is split, the register must reconcile old and new holder identities and values.

M04

Compliance extensions and failure permissions

Assess restricted transfer, freeze, forced transfer, upgrade, pause and recovery interfaces. Their existence is not evidence of appropriateness: emergency authority can be a necessary compliance feature and also a governance concentration risk. Decide who can operate each control, under what signed policy, with what notice and audit trail. ERC-7943 and ERC-7518 are useful research references for possible interface designs, but not regulatory approval or production assurance.

Two systems of record. Dotted vertical links require explicit legal, accounting and settlement reconciliation—not presumed on-chain equivalence.

Calculation / invariant

Normalized represented quantity = token balance × declared unit conversion; backing ratio = eligible independent backing units ÷ outstanding receipt entitlement units.

The model supports interoperable recordkeeping without implying that heterogeneous legal rights become fungible just because one token standard can represent them.

What needs testing.

  • T01

    Implement the same synthetic claim with ERC-1155, ERC-6909 and ERC-3525 representations; compare event volume, complexity and gas, not solely initial deployment.

  • T02

    Introduce a restricted holder transfer, split/merge and partial redemption; verify registrar and token balances after each event.

  • T03

    Test role revocation, contract upgrade, pause, recovery and external supply attestation using a threat-model worksheet.

06 / CONCLUSION

Research implication.

TRR should define the rights and reconciliation model independently of any ERC token interface. Token standards then become replaceable representations selected by lifecycle requirements, liquidity integration and controls. A controlled security token is a governed financial system, not just a specialized balance map.
